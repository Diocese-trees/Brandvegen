# Don't Sound Like AI - Unified Copy Linter (v1.1)

Run every cold email, follow-up, LinkedIn post, and profile headline against this linter before publishing or sending. Flag every violation, explain why it breaks the rule, and rewrite cleanly.

## 2. Banned Phrases & Synthetic Sentence Shapes

### Banned Openers, Closers & Follow-Up Clichés
- `In today's world` / `In today's fast-paced world`
- `quick question`, `honest question`, `quick one`
- `hope this email finds you well`, `dear sir/madam`
- `last chance`, `only today`, `act now`
- `just bumping this`, `circling back`, `following up`, `checking in`, `gentle reminder`, `haven't heard from you`, `any thoughts?`, `did you see my last email`

### Banned Synthetic Sentence Shapes
- "Most people do X. The few who win do Y."
- "Stop doing X. Start doing Y."
- "It's not X. It's Y."
- "Here's the truth: [obvious thing]."
- "If you're not doing X, you're already behind."
- "You don't need more X. You need Y."
- "This is the thing that decides everything."
- Fake urgency or scarcity unless a genuine capacity limit exists.
- Hedged cost lines ("a chance for human error", "can lead to missed opportunities", "risks things slipping through the cracks"). State what actually happens as a fact.

## 3. Channel-Specific Formatting & Tone Rules

Never mix Cold Email rules with LinkedIn Post rules:

| Rule Area | Cold Email (Module 2) | LinkedIn Post (Module 3) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Voice** | WhatsApp message to a smart friend running a company | Curious, analytical, practical ("my read", "I noticed", "it made me think") |
| **Length** | 74-80 words (under 90 max; greeting counted, P.S./sig excluded) | Short punchy lines; carousels: cover 15-25w, slides 40-55w, close 20-30w |
| **Formatting** | Plain text only: no bold, bullets, headings, em/en dashes, exclamation marks | Plain post body: no hyphens or dashes, no bold, no bullets, no AI phrases |
| **Links** | Zero links, attachments, GIFs, or calendar links before reply; `[domain.com]` in sig | Zero external links in post body; add link in first comment at +20 mins |
| **CTA** | One direct low-friction ask (`Reply with "yes"`) + `"nope"` opt-out (never `"stop"`) | Real experience-based closing question; NO hard CTA or begging for DMs |
| **Questions** | Never stack two questions in one email | One real closing question |

## 4. Cold Email 8 Pre-Send Audit Questions

1. Is there a banned word, phrase, or sentence shape in it?
2. Is any sentence longer than one breath when read out loud?
3. Is every number one you can stand behind and verify live?
4. Did you promise something you cannot actually deliver?
5. Would the reader know from the first sentence that this was written to them?
6. Does it read like a WhatsApp message to a smart colleague, or like marketing?
7. What is the exact word count (greeting counted; P.S. and signature excluded)? Target: 74-80 words.
8. **Which rule did I come closest to breaking?**

## 5. LinkedIn 11-Point Pre-Publish Audit

- [ ] Strategy: right pillar, 1 objective, 1 audience
- [ ] Truth: source log complete, fairness check passed, all `VERIFY` items cleared
- [ ] Content boundaries: zero hard-stop violations or exaggerated claims
- [ ] Voice: sounds genuinely human, zero banned words, zero dashes/bold/bullets
- [ ] Story: opens with a moment/observation, clear arc, practical takeaway
- [ ] Clarity: simple everyday English, 1 idea per slide or paragraph
- [ ] Save element: explicitly named and worth keeping
- [ ] 0.5-second test: visual stops a scrolling stranger instantly
- [ ] Word count: carousel slides within 15-25w / 40-55w / 20-30w budgets
- [ ] Format: correct size, author tag once, active kit colors, footer present
- [ ] Personal brand first: personal analysis leads; no company wordmark on personal graphics
