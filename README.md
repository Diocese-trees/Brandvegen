# Brandevegen

**Personal rebranding agent.**  
**Goal:** Get a job or land a client in 30 days.  
**Rule:** 90% hard work, 10% luck.

Brandevegen helps you rebuild how you present your skills, fix your outreach, and start real conversations with hiring managers or clients in 30 days.

## Why this exists

Most job searches and freelance pitches stall for three reasons:

1. **Design before clarity.** People redo their resume or portfolio before explaining what problem they solve.
2. **Robotic outreach.** Long emails packed with stiff phrases get deleted in three seconds.
3. **Empty claims.** Generic templates push people to inflate numbers they cannot back up on a call.

Brandevegen fixes this with four weekly checkpoints, a fact log, plain-text email rules, and ad scorecards.

## Pick your track

Both tracks follow the same 30-day plan, tuned to your target:

| What changes | Track A: Get a job | Track B: Land a client |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who you reach** | Hiring managers, founders, team leads, recruiters | Business owners, agency founders, department heads |
| **What you offer** | Job-ready skills backed by real work | A clear service plus a small free sample |
| **Where they check you** | LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio, resume, direct email | Landing page, past work, cold email, optional ads |
| **Next step** | Interview invite or referral to the right manager | Quick "yes" reply, free sample, then paid work |
| **What you track** | Replies, interviews booked, call feedback | Positive replies, samples sent, calls, proposals |

## How the 30 days work

Start with **Step 0 (Research Draft)**. List your real skills, finished work, and gaps before writing a single pitch.

1. **Days 1 to 7: Discover (Gate 1)**  
   Audit your skills, review your market, and separate facts from guesses.
2. **Days 8 to 14: Define (Gate 2)**  
   Pick one target group, write your positioning, and tie every claim to real proof. If you have no client numbers yet, build a small free sample instead.
3. **Days 15 to 21: Develop (Gate 3)**  
   Update your bio, LinkedIn, portfolio, resume, or service page so they match your positioning.
4. **Days 22 to 30: Deploy (Gate 4)**  
   Send plain-text outreach, track replies, and run weekly reviews for the next 90 days.

Do not move to the next week because the calendar says so. Move when the work for the current gate is done and verified.

## Core rules

- **Facts only.** Never invent numbers, client results, testimonials, or fake deadlines.
- **Short cold emails.** Keep Email 1 to 74-80 words (under 90 max). Pick one problem, one proof point, and one easy ask. Send plain text with no links or attachments until they reply.
- **Clean follow-ups.** Send 3 to 4 emails total, every other working day, in the same thread. End with one referral line asking who the right contact is.
- **No AI buzzwords.** Check every draft against `references/dont-sound-like-ai.md` before sending.
- **Score ads first (Track B).** Rate Google and Meta ad drafts out of 100 before spending money. Rewrite anything under 75.
