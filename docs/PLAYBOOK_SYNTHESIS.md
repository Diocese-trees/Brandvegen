# Brandevegen Playbook Synthesis & Scoring Reference (v1.1)

Unified operational rules, workflows, and scoring formulas synthesizing all four source playbooks.

## 1. Module 1: 30-Day Brand Sprint (4 Gates)

### Phase 1: Discover (Days 1 to 7) - Gate 1
- **Day 01 - Kickoff & Goal Charter:** Define Track A (target role/level/comp) or Track B (target service/pricing/niche), constraints, and assumptions log.
- **Day 02 - Skill & Offer Inventory:** Map hard skills, delivered projects, proof assets, and honest gaps.
- **Day 03 - Market & Category Scan:** Review current hiring signals, freelance pricing cues, buyer terms, and regional norms with dated sources.
- **Day 04 - Buyer / Hiring Discovery:** Extract exact language from job specs, hiring manager posts, reviews, or past call notes.
- **Day 05 - Alternative & Competitor Audit:** Map direct peers, agencies, and the status quo.
- **Day 06 - Opportunity & Tension Map:** Identify recurring friction where your skills create credible leverage.
- **Day 07 - Gate 1 Synthesis:** Separate facts, inferences, and unknowns. Decide: proceed, narrow niche, or gather targeted evidence.

### Phase 2: Define (Days 8 to 14) - Gate 2
- **Day 08 - Core Problem Statement:** Name the exact career/pipeline bottleneck before designing anything.
- **Day 09 - Audience Prioritization:** Score 2-3 segments (1-5) on problem intensity, offer fit, ability to pay/hire, reachability, competition, and delivery fit.
- **Day 10 - Category Frame & Parity Cues:** Confirm minimum legitimacy cues (clean GitHub, live demos, ATS-readable CV, clear service scope).
- **Day 11 - Positioning & Differentiation:** Test 2-3 options on 7 criteria (relevance, distinctiveness, credibility, competitive legitimacy, fit, durability, clarity). Weak credibility or fit is a stop-and-resolve blocker.
- **Day 12 - Proof Ladder & Claim Substantiation:** Pair every promise with verifiable proof, or build a zero-risk "free slice" offer.
- **Day 13 - Personal Brand Foundations:** Set ambition, 3-5 behavioral traits, desired associations, and explicit boundaries.
- **Day 14 - Gate 2 Review:** Lock the Personal Brand Strategy Blueprint.

### Phase 3: Develop (Days 15 to 21) - Gate 3
- **Day 15 - Messaging Architecture:** Core headline, value proposition, supporting pillars, proof lines, audience variants.
- **Day 16 - Voice & Verbal Identity:** Writing rules, buyer vocabulary, banned words.
- **Day 17 - Visual & Creative Direction:** Coherent visual kit grounded in strategy.
- **Day 18 - Core Identity Assets:** Consistent headshot, banner (`1584x396`), portfolio, resume, or service one-pager.
- **Day 19 - Touchpoint & Journey Mapping:** First impression -> profile/portfolio click -> reply -> call -> follow-up.
- **Day 20 - Prototype Touchpoints + First Proof Posts:** Draft LinkedIn Above-the-Fold, Portfolio Hero, Email 1, and first 2 LinkedIn Authority posts (Module 3).
- **Day 21 - Gate 3 Review:** Audit against 7 creative criteria (strategic fit, recognition, distinctiveness, legibility/accessibility, flexibility, feasibility, cultural/legal risk).

### Phase 4: Deploy (Days 22 to 30) - Gate 4
- **Day 22 - Launch Brief:** Lock 30-day outreach quota, LinkedIn posting days, and single-minded message.
- **Day 23 - Channel Plan:** Cold email (Sword) + LinkedIn Authority (Shield) + optional Track B Paid Ads (Accelerator).
- **Day 24 - Outreach & Content Pack:** Finalize linted Email 1-4 sequences, LinkedIn post queue, FAQs, and split tests.
- **Day 25 - Conversion & Call Enablement:** Test destination links/forms, prepare interview or discovery-call scripts.
- **Day 26 - Execution Calendar:** Lock daily prospecting (verify first 20 leads before scaling), sending, and 60-minute LinkedIn engagement windows.
- **Day 27 - Governance & Asset Storage:** Master folders, version control, swipe folder, Idea Bank.
- **Day 28 - Measurement & Baseline:** Replies, positive reply %, saves, profile views, DMs, interviews/calls booked.
- **Day 29 - Readiness & Risk Review:** Spam check, readability check, mobile check, link check.
- **Day 30 - Gate 4 Launch & 90-Day Rhythm:** Weeks 1-2 fix friction; Weeks 3-4 refine messaging; Month 2 double down on winning channels; Month 3 keep/adjust/test/retire.

## 2. Module 2: Cold Outreach Engine (The Sword)

- **Word count:** 74 to 80 words (ceiling under 90); count greeting, exclude P.S. and signature.
- **Subject line:** Under 50 chars, conversational, lowercase preferred, no clickbait/CAPS/emoji. Never use "quick question."
- **Opening:** Sentence 1 = question under 15 words (one breath). Sentence 2 = factual cost (~10 words; never "might" or "risks").
- **Proof:** 1 sentence, 1 verifiable number, client named only with permission (else anonymize). Name country only if relevant to recipient.
- **Free slice rule:** One genuinely useful slice that costs little to produce and reveals deeper work. Never give away the whole service.
- **CTA:** One low-friction ask (`Reply with "yes" and I'll send X`, or `Should I send it over here or on LinkedIn?`). No meeting ask or calendar link in Email 1.
- **P.S.:** Eye-tracking order: 1. Name -> 2. Signature -> 3. P.S. -> 4. Body. Opt-out uses `nope` (never `stop`).
- **Follow-ups:** 3-4 emails total, every other working day, same thread. Standalone, shorter than prior email, new proof/angle. Final email on one line: `Hey [Name], is there anyone better at the company to reach out to about this?`
- **Deliverability:** Plain text only, plain signature with `[domain.com]`, zero links/attachments before reply, do NOT track opens, stop only for the person who asks.
- **Priority split tests:** 1. Offer, 2. Opening line (`Wild guess, but...` vs `Do you still...?`), 3. Subject line (noun+company vs noun), 4. Language (50/50 local vs English if no native speaker).

## 3. Module 3: LinkedIn Authority Engine (The Shield - v2.4)

### The 13-Stage Execution Workflow
1. **Idea Intake** -> Idea Card (Topic, Why now, Source material, Sensitivity, Verification needed).
2. **Classify** -> Pick 1 of 4 locked pillars (never invent a 5th).
3. **Objective** -> Pick 1: `Authority`, `Trust`, `Reach`, `Leads`, or `Hiring`.
4. **Research** -> Run pillar checklist + Source Log (claim, source, date, confidence) + Fairness Check + `VERIFY` flags.
5. **Angle** -> Draft 5 angles (`Decision`, `Contradiction`, `Pattern`, `Mistake`, `Lesson`), pick 1.
6. **Hook** -> Draft 5 hooks (`Curiosity gap`, `Pattern interrupt`, `Identity`, `Loss aversion`, `Observation`), pick 1 passing the **0.5-second test**.
7. **Story** -> `Moment -> Tension -> Evidence -> Insight -> Takeaway -> Real closing question` + explicitly named **Save element**.
8. **Format** -> Pillar default unless a clear reason overrides it.
9. **Write** -> Personal brand first ("my read", "what I noticed"), short everyday English, channel-clean rules.
10. **Design** -> Designer brief + AI image generation prompt using active visual kit.
11. **Review** -> 11-point scorecard (100% pass required; any failure triggers fix and re-run).
12. **Schedule** -> 2x/week slot from monthly 8-post sequence.
13. **Publish + Learn** -> 60-minute engagement window + 48-hour metrics logged to Idea Bank.

### The 4 Locked Pillars & Pillar Research Checklists

| Pillar | Default Format | Size / Length | Mandatory Research Checklist |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Founder Spotlight** | Single image | `1080x1080` | Founding moment/problem, 1 decisive choice, what they risked, verified early traction, real interview quotes only, founder confirmation where possible, plain lesson. |
| **Marketing Breakdown** | Carousel | `1080x1350` (8-12 slides) | Dated campaign/growth move, target audience & channel, message/offer/creative, business impact after, competitor comparison, public sourced results only, copyable lesson. |
| **Brand Breakdown** | Carousel | `1080x1350` (8-12 slides) | 7 lenses (Brand, Audience, Positioning, Communication, Psychology, Competition, Evidence), homepage/packaging/social/ads, 1 sharp finding, 1 direct competitor, fairness check (what they do well), 1 practical fix. |
| **Cheat Sheet** | Single image | `1080x1080` | 1-sentence core idea, 3-5 cross-checked trusted sources, ordered testable steps, 1 real example per step, common mistakes, local market context where relevant. |

### Format Specs, Word Limits & Cadence Patch
- **Carousel word budgets:** Cover `15-25` words (0.5-second hook) | Content slides `40-55` words (1 idea/slide, reason to swipe) | Closing slide `20-30` words (takeaway + question). Cap at **3 carousels/month** to protect research depth.
- **Monthly 8-post sequence (Reconciled):**
  - *30-Day Sprint Mode (Strict 1 Cheat Sheet cap):* 3 Founder Spotlights, 2 Brand Breakdowns, 2 Marketing Breakdowns, 1 Cheat Sheet (slots: 1 FS, 2 BB, 3 CS, 4 MB, 5 FS, 6 BB, 7 MB, 8 FS).
  - *Steady-State Mode (2 Cheat Sheets allowed):* 3 Founder Spotlights, 2 Cheat Sheets, 2 Marketing Breakdowns, 1 Brand Breakdown.
- **Profile Banner:** `1584x396` (profile only, never a post).

### 9-Stage Reader Psychology & Pillar Mechanics
1. **Stop** (0.5s high-contrast visual hook) -> 2. **Stay** (curiosity gap in first 2 lines) -> 3. **Read** (short sentences, open loops / Zeigarnik payoff) -> 4. **Save** (named saveable framework/checklist/rule) -> 5. **Share** (identity relevance) -> 6. **Comment** (real experience-based closing question) -> 7. **Visit profile** (consistent POV) -> 8. **Follow** (consistent series & days) -> 9. **DM** (earned trust, never begged).
- *Pillar specifics:* Founder Spotlight (open loop on cover, close on final line); Marketing Breakdown (each slide earns next swipe); Brand Breakdown (label finding on visual, explain why in copy); Cheat Sheet (numbered tool worth keeping).

### 11-Point Pre-Publish Review Checklist (All 11 Must Pass)
1. **Strategy:** Right pillar, 1 objective, 1 audience.
2. **Truth:** Source log complete, fairness check done, all `VERIFY` flags cleared.
3. **Content boundaries (Hard Stop):** No interest-based finance recommendations, alcohol, gambling, inappropriate imagery, exaggeration, false news, fake promises, fake stories, or violations of user's declared values.
4. **Voice:** Sounds like the person, zero banned AI words, no dashes/bold/bullets in post body.
5. **Story:** Opens with a moment, clear arc, usable takeaway.
6. **Clarity:** Simple English, 1 idea per slide or paragraph.
7. **Save element:** Explicitly named and worth keeping.
8. **0.5-second test:** Stranger stops on the visual immediately.
9. **Word count:** Cover 15-25w, content 40-55w, closing 20-30w (for carousels).
10. **Format:** Correct dimensions, author tag once, active kit colors, footer present.
11. **Personal brand first:** Personal analysis leads; no company wordmark on personal graphics.

### 60-Minute Engineering Window & 48-Hour Learning Loop
- **Pre-post:** DM 3 relevant people who genuinely care about the topic.
- **+20 minutes:** Post the first comment (place any reference link here, never in post body).
- **Within 60 minutes:** Comment thoughtfully on 10 relevant posts; reply to every comment on your post.
- **At +48 hours:** Log 6 learning metrics (First 60-min response, Comments, Saves [main signal], Profile views, Follower gain, DMs). Record 1 win, 1 fix, and turn comment/DM questions into new Idea Cards.

### Optional Reference Preset (Advegen / Founder Kit - Load Only If Requested)
- **Schedule:** Tuesday & Saturday, 11:00 AM PKT.
- **Colors:** Deep purple `#3B1F6B`, white, charcoal `#1A1A1A`, cream `#F5F0EA` (Founder Spotlight covers only), dark `#1A0A2E` (final carousel slide only). Avoid amber, gradients, glow, light purple.
- **Fonts:** League Spartan (ExtraBold/Bold headlines), Manrope (Regular/Medium/SemiBold body).
- **Author tag:** Circular photo with purple border, no pill, `"Name / Role"`, top-left on covers/single images, bottom-left on carousel interior slides (once per graphic).
- **Footer:** `"I Study The Decisions That Build Brands."` in small caps.

## 4. Module 4: Paid Advertising Quick Rules & 100-Point Rubrics (Track B Optional)

### Shared 7-Step Foundation
1. Objective -> 2. Audience -> 3. Offer & terms -> 4. Differentiator -> 5. Message & verified proof -> 6. Destination match -> 7. KPI & tracking before launch.

### Platform Specs & Weighted Formula
- **Google RSA:** Up to 15 headlines (30 chars max), 4 descriptions (90 chars max). Intent -> Relevance -> Value Prop -> Action.
- **Meta Ads:** Hook -> Relevance -> Value/Evidence -> CTA across 5 angles (Problem, Outcome, Demonstration, Trust, Offer).
- **Formula:** Rate 1-5 per criterion; `Weighted Points = Rating x Weight / 5`; sum to 100.
- **Bands:** 90-100 Excellent | 75-89 Strong | 60-74 Needs improvement (Revise) | <60 Rewrite.

| Google Ads Criterion | Weight | Meta Ads Criterion | Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Search intent alignment | 20 | Hook and attention | 15 |
| Keyword and ad relevance | 15 | Audience relevance | 15 |
| Value proposition | 15 | Value proposition | 15 |
| Headline variety and quality | 10 | Offer clarity | 10 |
| Description clarity | 10 | Creative and copy alignment | 10 |
| Landing page alignment | 10 | Message clarity and readability | 10 |
| CTA specificity | 5 | CTA relevance | 10 |
| Trust and claim accuracy | 5 | Credibility and evidence | 5 |
| Technical and policy compliance | 5 | Landing page or destination alignment | 5 |
| Offer clarity | 5 | Platform policy and technical compliance | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** | **Total** | **100** |
