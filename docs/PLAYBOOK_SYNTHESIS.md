# Brandevegen Playbook Synthesis & Scoring Reference

Unified operational rules and scoring formulas synthesizing all three source playbooks into a 30-day personal rebranding system.

---

## 1. Day-by-Day 30-Day Delivery Schedule (Personal Rebranding)

### Phase 1: Discover (Days 1 to 7) - Gate 1
- **Day 01 - Kickoff & Goal Charter:** Define Track A (target role/level/comp) or Track B (target service/pricing/client niche), constraints, and assumptions log.
- **Day 02 - Skill & Offer Inventory:** Map hard skills, delivered projects, strengths, proof assets, and honest gaps. No positioning claim may rest on an unverified skill.
- **Day 03 - Market & Category Scan:** Review current hiring signals, freelance pricing cues, industry terms, and regional norms. Log dated sources.
- **Day 04 - Buyer / Hiring Discovery:** Extract exact language from job posts, hiring manager posts, client reviews, or past interview/sales notes.
- **Day 05 - Alternative & Competitor Audit:** Map direct peers, agencies, and the "do nothing / hire internally" status quo.
- **Day 06 - Opportunity & Tension Map:** Pinpoint recurring friction where your specific skill set creates credible leverage.
- **Day 07 - Gate 1 Synthesis:** Separate facts, inferences, and unknowns. Decide: proceed, narrow niche, or gather targeted extra evidence.

### Phase 2: Define (Days 8 to 14) - Gate 2
- **Day 08 - Core Problem Statement:** Write the exact career/pipeline bottleneck without prematurely jumping to "I need a new portfolio design."
- **Day 09 - Target Audience Prioritization:** Score 2 to 3 target segments (1-5) on problem intensity, offer fit, willingness/ability to pay or hire, reachability, competition, and delivery fit.
- **Day 10 - Category Frame & Parity Cues:** Confirm minimum expectations needed to be taken seriously (clean GitHub, live demos, ATS-readable CV, clear service scope).
- **Day 11 - Positioning & Differentiation:** Draft 2-3 positioning angles and stress-test against the 7 positioning criteria (relevance, distinctiveness, credibility, competitive legitimacy, business fit, durability, clarity). Weak credibility or business fit is a stop-and-resolve blocker.
- **Day 12 - Proof Ladder & Claim Substantiation:** Link every claim to verifiable evidence. Design the zero-risk "free slice" offer if numerical case studies are missing.
- **Day 13 - Personal Brand Foundations:** Set ambition, 3-5 behavioral personality traits, desired associations, and explicit boundaries (what you will NOT do or claim).
- **Day 14 - Gate 2 Review:** Lock the Personal Brand Strategy Blueprint before writing launch copy or redesigning touchpoints.

### Phase 3: Develop (Days 15 to 21) - Gate 3
- **Day 15 - Messaging Architecture:** Build core headline, value proposition, supporting pillars, proof lines, and audience-specific variants.
- **Day 16 - Voice & Verbal Identity:** Translate voice traits into concrete writing rules, buyer vocabulary, and banned words.
- **Day 17 - Visual & Creative Direction:** Choose 1 coherent visual direction (typography, color restraint, layout hierarchy) grounded in strategy, not trends.
- **Day 18 - Core Identity Assets:** Finalize headshot consistency, banner, portfolio structure, resume layout, or service one-pager.
- **Day 19 - Touchpoint & Journey Mapping:** Map the full buyer/recruiter path: first impression -> profile/portfolio click -> reply -> call -> follow-up.
- **Day 20 - Prototype Priority Touchpoints:** Draft live prototypes of LinkedIn Above-the-Fold, Portfolio Hero, and Cold Email 1.
- **Day 21 - Gate 3 Creative Review:** Audit prototypes against the 7 creative criteria (strategic fit, recognition potential, distinctiveness, legibility/accessibility, flexibility, feasibility, cultural/legal risk).

### Phase 4: Deploy (Days 22 to 30) - Gate 4
- **Day 22 - Launch Brief:** Lock 30-day outreach target, daily activity quota, and single-minded message.
- **Day 23 - Channel & Distribution Plan:** Pick 1-2 primary channels (cold email + LinkedIn DM, or Google/Meta ads for Track B) based on buyer behavior and bandwidth.
- **Day 24 - Outreach & Content Pack:** Finalize Email 1-4 sequences (linted), FAQs, objection handlers, and 2-3 split tests.
- **Day 25 - Conversion & Call Enablement:** Verify landing page/portfolio links, response workflows, and interview/discovery call scripts.
- **Day 26 - Execution Calendar:** Lock daily prospecting, enrichment (always verify the first 20 leads before scaling), sending, and follow-up schedule.
- **Day 27 - Governance & Asset Storage:** Organize master files, version control, and reusable swipe folder.
- **Day 28 - Measurement & Baseline:** Define KPIs (replies, positive reply %, interviews/calls booked, conversion friction) with weekly review cadence.
- **Day 29 - Readiness & Risk Review:** Run spam check, readability check, mobile rendering check, and link/form verification.
- **Day 30 - Gate 4 Launch & 90-Day Rhythm:** Go live and schedule post-launch checkpoints (Weeks 1-2 fix friction, Weeks 3-4 refine messaging, Month 2 shift toward winning channels, Month 3 keep/adjust/test/retire).

## 2. Cold Outreach Quick Rules (Module 2)

- **Word count:** 74 to 80 words (ceiling under 90); count greeting, exclude P.S. and signature.
- **Subject line:** Under 50 chars, conversational, lowercase preferred, no clickbait/CAPS/emoji. Never use "quick question."
- **Opening:** Sentence 1 = question under 15 words (one breath). Sentence 2 = factual cost (~10 words; never "might" or "risks").
- **Proof:** 1 sentence, 1 verifiable number, client named only with permission (otherwise anonymize). Name country only if relevant to the lead.
- **Free slice rule:** Give away one genuinely useful slice that costs little to produce and reveals the deeper work. Never give away the whole service or something they can do faster themselves.
- **CTA:** One low-friction ask (`Reply with "yes" and I'll send X`, or two-way yes: `Should I send it over here or on LinkedIn?`). No meeting ask or calendar link in Email 1.
- **P.S.:** Eye-tracking read order: 1. Name -> 2. Signature -> 3. P.S. -> 4. Body. Include an opt-out using "nope" (never "stop").
- **Follow-ups:** 3 to 4 emails total, every other working day, same thread. Each standalone, shorter than the last, bringing new proof or angle. Final email on one line: `Hey [Name], is there anyone better at the company to reach out to about this?`
- **Deliverability:** Plain text only, plain signature with `[domain.com]`, zero links/attachments before reply, do NOT track opens (tracking pixels hurt deliverability), stop only for the person who asks (not company-wide).
- **Split tests to prioritize:** 1. Offer (moves numbers most), 2. Opening line (`Wild guess, but...` vs `Do you still...?`), 3. Subject line (noun+company vs noun), 4. Language (50/50 local vs English if no native speaker).

## 3. Paid Advertising Quick Rules & 100-Point Rubrics (Module 3 - Track B)

### Shared 7-Step Foundation
1. Define objective. 2. Research audience. 3. Define exact offer & terms. 4. Identify credible differentiator. 5. Choose primary message & verified evidence. 6. Match destination page promise. 7. Define KPI & conversion tracking before launch.

### Platform Specs & Frameworks
- **Google Search Ads (RSA):** Up to 15 headlines (30 chars hard limit) and 4 descriptions (90 chars hard limit). Count finished text, not templates. Structure: Search Intent -> Relevance -> Value Proposition -> Action.
- **Meta Ads (FB/IG):** Structure: Hook -> Relevance -> Value & Evidence -> Call to Action. Templates: Problem & Solution, Benefit-led, Demonstration or Proof, Offer-led. Vary 5 strategic angles: Problem, Outcome, Demonstration, Trust, Offer.

### Weighted Scoring Formula (Both Platforms)
Rate each criterion 1 to 5 (1 Weak, 2 Below standard, 3 Acceptable, 4 Strong, 5 Excellent). Calculate: `Weighted Points = Rating x Weight / 5`. Sum all 10 criteria for total out of 100.

**Bands:** 90-100 Excellent | 75-89 Strong | 60-74 Needs improvement (Revise) | Below 60 Rewrite.

| Google Ads Criterion | Weight | Meta Ads Criterion | Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Search intent alignment | 20 | Hook and attention | 15 |
| Keyword and ad relevance | 15 | Audience relevance | 15 |
| Value proposition | 15 | Value proposition | 15 |
| Headline variety and quality | 10 | Offer clarity | 10 |
| Description clarity | 10 | Creative and copy alignment | 10 |
| Landing page alignment | 10 | Message clarity and readability | 10 |
| CTA specificity | 5 | CTA relevance | 10 |
| Trust and claim accuracy | 5 | Credibility and evidence | 5 |
| Technical and policy compliance | 5 | Landing page or destination alignment | 5 |
| Offer clarity | 5 | Platform policy and technical compliance | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** | **Total** | **100** |

### Post-Launch Optimization SOP (Apply Patience Rule)
- **Low CTR:** Check query relevance & ad messaging (Google) or hook, creative relevance, offer, and audience fit (Meta).
- **High clicks, low conversions:** Audit intent/targeting, landing page match, page speed, offer consistency, and tracking.
- **High lead volume, low quality:** Tighten search terms/audience, lead form qualification questions, and follow-up speed.
- **High CPA:** Audit conversion rate, bidding/objective, traffic quality, and funnel friction.
- **Meta early wins then decline:** Refresh creative to address creative fatigue, audience saturation, and frequency.
