## Template 5: Cold Email Sequence Pack (Module 2)

### Email 1 (Target: 74 to 80 words excluding P.S. and signature)

```text
Subject: [under 50 chars, conversational, lowercase preferred]

Hey [FirstName],

Do you still [specific problem under 15 words]? It [factual cost, ~10 words].

I [one sentence on how you fixed this for a client/project, with 1 verified number].

Happy to [small free slice you can deliver fast], for free.

Reply with "yes" and I'll send you the next steps.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Role/Focus] [[yourdomain.com]]

P.S. If this isn't relevant, reply with "nope" and I won't send you more free stuff.
```

### Zero-Proof Cold-Start Variant (When You Have No Client Metric Yet)

```text
I can build [specific small slice] for you. There's no risk on your side, you don't pay and you don't spend time on it. I'll do all of it, if you let me use it as a case study afterwards.
```

### Follow-Up 2 & 3 (Every other working day, same thread, standalone, shorter than prior email)
- Bring a fresh proof point, micro-teardown observation, or alternate angle on the same problem.
- Zero links, zero attachments, zero "just following up."

### Final Email (Step 3 or Step 4 - One Line Only)

```text
Hey [FirstName], is there anyone better at [Company] to reach out to about this?
```

| [YYYY-MM-DD] | [Choice made] | [Why + what was sacrificed] | [Name] | [Approved] | [What data reverses it] |

### 90-Day Post-Launch Rhythm

| Window | What to Review | Decision Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weeks 1 to 2** | Broken links, spam placement, message clarity, early objections | Fix critical friction and broken promises first |
| **Weeks 3 to 4** | Reply rates, interview/call conversion, split-test winners | Refine positioning, offer slice, or outreach angle on data |
| **Month 2** | Audience fit, best-performing channel, recurring buyer questions | Shift effort toward evidence-backed winning channels |
| **Month 3** | Commercial/career outcomes, delivery consistency, initial assumptions | Keep, adjust, test, or retire tactics; set next 90-day goal |
